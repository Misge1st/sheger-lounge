Our story

A modern Ethiopian table

Elo Restaurant brings the heat of Kazanchis cuisine to a polished dining room near Urael — where meat is cut to order, fasting plates stay true, and hospitality comes first.

We cook for lunch crowds and late dinners alike: shekla and gas light by the kilo, kitfo and firfir for everyday cravings, and yetsom plates when the calendar calls for fasting.

Order for delivery, stop in for the full plate experience, or browse photos of dishes before you choose.
